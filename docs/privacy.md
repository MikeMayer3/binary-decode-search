# Privacy Policy for Binary Decode & Search

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Binary Decode & Search does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or browsing information.

When you choose "Decode binary and search Google" from the right-click menu, the extension decodes the text you selected on your own device and opens a new tab with a Google search for the decoded text. That search is sent to Google only because you asked for it, exactly as if you had typed it into Google yourself, and is covered by Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). The extension itself never sees, saves, or sends anything else.

The extension does not use cookies, analytics, tracking, or any servers of its own, and it does not read the pages you visit.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/MikeMayer3/binary-decode-search/issues
